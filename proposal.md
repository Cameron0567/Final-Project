## Description
    # For my final project, I will create a destop shimeji or pet that wanders around the user's desktop. 
    This is relevant to media and digital arts by being a fun pick me up when stressed and working on your laptop/computer.

## Features
    # Feature 1: Shimeji can walk around the destop area.
    # Feature 2: Shimeji can climb, crawl, be picked up, dragged around, and dropped with mouse click.
    # Feature 3: Shimeji has different sprites when performing actions.
